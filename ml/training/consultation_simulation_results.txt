HOW TO READ THIS
======================================================================
'named' = the app showed a condition. 'uncertain_*' = it showed the
Symptoms Unclear card instead. A high uncertain rate is not a failure
in itself - it means the gates are refusing thin evidence - but if the
25-class model almost never names anything, the extra diseases add
little in practice and that must be reported honestly.

Caveat: a simulated user answers every follow-up truthfully from the
dataset row. Real users misremember, and Whisper mistranscribes, so
these numbers are an optimistic ceiling, not expected field accuracy.
