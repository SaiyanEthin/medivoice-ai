READING THIS
==============================================================================
named%    - how often the app shows a condition instead of Symptoms Unclear
correct%  - of those, how many were right
wrong     - count of confidently WRONG answers; this is the number that
            matters most for safety, and it should stay at zero
unreachable - diseases never named even once across all simulations

Lowering the threshold raises coverage but weakens the evidence needed
to name a condition. More rounds means more questions for the user to
answer, which is a UX cost rather than a safety one. Simulated users
answer perfectly, so these are optimistic ceilings.
